Help & troubleshooting

Yellow "Sign in again" banner showing?
Click Sign in with Google at the top. That's the whole fix.

A service didn't go public?
Check the top says Signed in ✓ and Auto-publish ON. You can also set any stream to Public by hand in YouTube Studio.

Page won't load, or "server IP address not found"?
That's the internet, not this tool. Check the Wi-Fi, then refresh (Ctrl/Cmd + R).

"Google hasn't verified this app" screen?
This is our own church app — it's safe. Click Advanced → Go to King's Anchorage.
